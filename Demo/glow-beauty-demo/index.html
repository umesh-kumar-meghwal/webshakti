Bridal tips

5 bridal makeup tips for a long-lasting look

Prep your skin a week early, choose a primer that suits your skin type, set with a fine powder and carry a small touch-up kit. Always do a trial a month before the wedding.

Skin care

How to prepare your skin before the wedding

Start three months ahead with a simple routine: cleanse, moisturise, use sunscreen and book facials at regular gaps. Avoid trying new products in the last two weeks.
