About Our School

Our story

A village school with a big dream

Mahatma Ghani Vidya Niketan School in Sangod started with one idea: every child deserves quality education close to home. Over the years we have grown from a few classrooms into a full campus with labs, a library and playgrounds.

We follow a simple formula: strong basics, steady discipline, and plenty of encouragement.

Our vision

To raise confident, honest and capable citizens who make Sangod and India proud.

Our mission

To give every child quality teaching, a safe campus and the chance to discover their own talent.
