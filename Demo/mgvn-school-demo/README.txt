MAHATMA GHANI VIDYA NIKETAN SCHOOL, SANGOD - WEBSITE

How to run: unzip and open index.html in a browser. Internet is needed (Bootstrap, Tailwind, fonts and photos load from CDN).

Change school details: open js/main.js and edit the SCHOOL block at the top (phone, WhatsApp number, email, address).
Change photos: replace the Unsplash links in the html files with your own image paths (example: images/school1.jpg).
Pages: index, about, academics, admissions, gallery, events, contact.
Upload: put all files in public_html of your hosting.
