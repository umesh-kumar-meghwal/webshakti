Frequently asked questions

How long is the first visit?

About 45-60 minutes including assessment and first treatment.

Can I reschedule?

Yes, message us on WhatsApp two hours before.

How many sessions will I need?

It varies. After your assessment we share an estimate and review it every few sessions.

What should I wear?

Comfortable, loose clothing that lets us see and move the painful area.

Do you offer home visits?

Yes, anywhere in Kota city. Call or WhatsApp to book.
