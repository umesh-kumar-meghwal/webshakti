E-commerce Development FAQs

How much does an e-commerce website cost in Kota?

The cost depends on the number of products, payment and shipping integrations and whether you need multi-vendor features. We quote each store after a short discussion, so you only pay for what your business needs.

Which payment gateways do you integrate?

We integrate popular Indian gateways such as Razorpay, PayU, Cashfree and Paytm, with UPI, cards, net banking, wallets and Cash on Delivery options.

Can you build a multi-vendor marketplace?

Yes. We build marketplaces where each vendor has a separate panel to add products, manage stock and orders, while you control approvals, commission and payouts.

Is online payment on my store safe?

Yes. Card details are handled by the payment gateway's secure page, not stored on your server. We also use SSL, signature verification, CSRF protection and validated inputs.

How long does it take to build an online store?

A basic store usually takes 3-5 weeks. A multi-vendor marketplace with custom features takes longer, depending on the scope.

Will my online store rank on Google?

We build the store with SEO-friendly URLs, fast loading, product schema and mobile-first design. Rankings also depend on content and competition, so we offer SEO and digital marketing after launch.
