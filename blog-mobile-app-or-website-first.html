Mobile App or Website First? A Simple Guide for Small Businesses

"Everyone has a phone, so I need an app" is a common thought. It is not always right. Apps are powerful but costly, and customers must install them first. Use these questions to decide.

By Web Shakti Team 18 Oct 2026 3 min read

Start with a website for most businesses

A website works on every phone and computer without installation. It is found through Google, shared on WhatsApp in one tap and costs far less than an app. For salons, coaching classes, clinics, shops and service providers, a fast mobile-friendly website is the right first step.

When an app makes sense

  • People use it every day or every week. Examples: delivery, learning, fitness, attendance.
  • You need push notifications to bring customers back.
  • You need device features such as camera, GPS tracking or offline access.
  • You run a loyalty or membership program where customers log in often.
  • You already have a steady customer base that will install it.

Website vs app at a glance

FactorWebsiteMobile app
Found on GoogleYesLimited
Customer must installNoYes

Push notificationsLimitedYes

Works across Android and iPhoneYesDepends on how it is built

A smart phased plan

  1. Phase 1: website. Launch with your services, contact and WhatsApp, and get found on Google.
  2. Phase 2: web application features. Add logins, bookings or an admin panel if customers need them.
  3. Phase 3: mobile app. Once you see repeat use and clear demand, build an app that does what customers already do on the web.

This order protects your budget. You learn what customers actually want before you invest in an app.

Five questions before you build an app: Who will install it? How often will they open it? What can it do that a website cannot? How will people hear about it? Who will maintain it after launch?
