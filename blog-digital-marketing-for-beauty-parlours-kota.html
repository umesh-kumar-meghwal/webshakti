Digital Marketing for Beauty Parlours in Kota

Digital marketing for a parlour is not about big ads. It is about being easy to find, easy to trust and easy to book.

A website, a complete Google Business Profile and a WhatsApp number. These three bring most local enquiries.

Add regular content

Two or three Instagram posts or reels a week: before and after looks, offers and festival packages. Reuse the same photos on your website gallery.
