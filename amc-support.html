What is AMC

Why a Website Needs Ongoing Care After Launch

AMC (Annual Maintenance Contract) is a yearly plan where we look after your website or app so you do not have to. Think of it like servicing a car: a launch day website runs perfectly, but without regular care it slowly breaks down.

Frameworks release security fixes, servers change, browsers and phones get new versions, and hackers constantly look for old, unpatched sites. A website that is left alone can become slow, show errors, lose Google ranking or get hacked, and the cost of repair is much higher than the cost of prevention.

With AMC, problems are prevented or fixed early, your data is always backed up, and you have a real team to call when something goes wrong.

Security patches & updates

Stay Ahead of Hackers, Not Behind Them

Most hacked websites were not targeted personally. They were running old software with a known weakness. When a framework such as Laravel or Django publishes a security fix, attackers start scanning for sites that have not applied it.

We check for updates regularly, test them on a safe copy of your site first and then apply them, so you get the protection without surprise breakages.

  • Framework, library and plugin updates
  • Malware scans and suspicious file checks
  • SSL certificate renewal and HTTPS checks
  • Firewall rules, login protection and strong passwords
  • PHP and server version upgrades

AMC & Support FAQs

What is a website AMC?

AMC stands for Annual Maintenance Contract. You pay a fixed amount for a year, and we keep your website or app updated, secure, backed up and bug-free, with technical support when you need it.

Why do I need AMC if my website is already built?

Software ages. Frameworks, plugins and servers need regular updates and security patches, and a website without maintenance can slow down, break or get hacked. AMC prevents these problems instead of fixing them after damage is done.

How do your cloud backups work?

We take scheduled automatic backups of your website files and database, store them encrypted in cloud storage separate from your server, and keep multiple restore points so we can bring your site back quickly if something goes wrong.

Is 24/7 support included?

Support hours and response times depend on the plan you choose. Our priority plans include round-the-clock coverage for critical issues, and the exact terms are written in your agreement.

Do you maintain websites that you did not build?

Yes, after a short technical audit. We review the code, hosting and security first and then tell you honestly what it needs before we take over maintenance.

Does AMC cover new features?

AMC covers maintenance, updates, backups, monitoring, bug fixes and support. Large new features or redesigns are quoted separately, while small changes can be included depending on the plan.

What happens if my website goes down?

Our monitoring alerts us, we investigate the cause, restore the site from a backup or fix the fault, and then share a short report on what happened and how we prevent it in future.
