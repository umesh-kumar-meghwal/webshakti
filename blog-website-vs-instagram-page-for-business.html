Website vs Instagram Page: What Does Your Business Actually Need?

Many small businesses run entirely on Instagram and wonder if a website is worth it. Instagram is excellent for showing your work, but it is rented space. Here is a fair comparison.

By Web Shakti Team 30 Oct 2026 3 min read

The core difference

On Instagram you are building on someone else's platform. The rules, reach and even your account can change overnight. A website is a home you own: you decide the design, the message and the way customers contact you.

QuestionInstagram pageWebsite
Found on Google searches?RarelyYes, with good SEO
Who controls it?The platformYou
Shows full prices, packages, FAQsHard to organiseEasy and permanent
Builds trust with new customersGood with reels and reviewsVery strong, looks established
Takes enquiries and bookingsThrough DMsForms, WhatsApp, call, maps
CostFree, but needs daily timeOne-time build plus small yearly cost

Where Instagram wins

Showing results fast. A mehndi artist, makeup artist or gym trainer can post work every day and build a following. Reels reach people who were not searching for you.

Where a website wins

  • Google search. People who search "mehndi artist in Kota" or "coaching near me" land on websites and map listings, not on your reels.
  • Trust. Parents checking a school, patients choosing a clinic and brides comparing artists all look for a proper website.
  • Clarity. Services, packages, timings, location and reviews sit in one place.
  • Ownership. If your account is hacked or reach drops, your website stays.

The best answer: use both

Treat Instagram as the shop window and your website as the shop. Post work on Instagram, put your website link in your bio, and send people there for details and bookings. On the website, add your Instagram feed link so visitors can see fresh work.

What your first website should include

  1. A clear headline saying what you do and where
  2. Services with starting prices or packages
  3. A gallery of real work
  4. Customer reviews
  5. WhatsApp and call buttons that work on mobile
  6. Address with Google Map

Tip: keep it simple. A fast five-page website that loads well on a phone beats a heavy one with many effects.
