Google Business Profile Checklist: How to Show Up in Local Searches

When someone searches "salon near me" or "coaching in Kota", the map results appear before most websites. Your Google Business Profile decides whether you are in that list. Work through this checklist once, then keep it fresh.

By Web Shakti Team 5 Nov 2026 3 min read

Set up the basics

  1. Claim and verify your profile. Search your business on Google Maps and claim it, or create a new one. Finish the verification so you can edit.
  2. Use your real business name. Add exactly what is on your signboard. Stuffing keywords or the city into the name can get the profile suspended.
  3. Pick the right primary category. This is one of the strongest signals. Choose the most specific one, such as "Makeup artist" rather than "Beauty salon" if that is your main work.
  4. Add secondary categories for other services you really offer.
  5. Enter an accurate address or, if you travel to customers, set your service area.
  6. Add your phone number and website link. Use a number that you answer.

Make it complete

  1. Set opening hours, including special hours for festivals and holidays.
  2. Write a clear description. Say what you do, who you serve and where, in natural language.
  3. List your services or products with short descriptions. Add prices where possible.
  4. Upload real photos. Your shop front, team, work samples and inside view. Fresh photos each month signal an active business.

Build trust and keep it active

  1. Ask happy customers for reviews. Share your review link on WhatsApp after a job. Never buy reviews.
  2. Reply to every review, good or bad, politely and briefly.
  3. Post updates about offers, events and new work.
  4. Keep your name, address and phone identical on your website, Instagram, directories and Google.

Remember: no one can guarantee a rank on Google Maps. What you can control is accuracy, completeness and activity, and those are what the checklist above covers.

Connect your profile to your website

Your profile and website work as a pair. Link your website from the profile, and add your Google Map, address and phone number to the website contact page. A page written for your city, such as a Kota or Jabalpur landing page, helps Google understand where you operate. See how we do this on our Jabalpur page.

How often should you update it?

Once a month is enough: add two or three photos, check hours, answer new reviews and post one update. It takes about fifteen minutes.
