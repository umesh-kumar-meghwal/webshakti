Privacy Policy

How Web Shakti handles information when you visit our website or contact us.

Last updated: 22 November 2026

Web Shakti (“we”, “our” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit webshakti.net.in or contact us about our services.

1. Information you provide

When you contact us, you may choose to provide details such as your name, phone number, email address, business information and project requirements. Please share only information that is necessary for your enquiry.

2. Technical information

Our website host and third-party tools may automatically process basic technical information, such as browser type, device information, pages visited, referring pages and access times. This information may be used to maintain website security, understand site performance and improve the visitor experience.

3. How we use information

  • To respond to enquiries and discuss requested services.
  • To prepare quotations and provide services you request.
  • To maintain, secure and improve our website.
  • To prevent misuse and comply with applicable legal obligations.

4. Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may use cookies or similar technologies for essential functionality, analytics and advertising. You can control cookies through your browser settings. If Google AdSense or other advertising services are enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to applicable settings and consent requirements. Learn more at Google’s advertising technologies information.

5. Third-party services and links

We may rely on service providers for website hosting, analytics, communication, forms or other functions. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Links to external websites are provided for convenience; we are not responsible for their privacy practices.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell your personal information. Information may be shared with service providers where needed to operate our website or deliver a requested service, or where disclosure is required by law. We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for its purpose or as required by law.

7. Security and your choices

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure. You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted or request correction or deletion where applicable. We may need to retain some records for legal or legitimate business purposes.

8. Children’s privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

We may update this page from time to time. The latest version and its effective date will be published here.
