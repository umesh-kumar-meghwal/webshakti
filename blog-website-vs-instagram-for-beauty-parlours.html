Website vs Instagram for Beauty Parlours

Instagram helps customers discover your parlour. A website helps them decide and book. The best parlours use both.

By Web Shakti Team 10 Nov 2026 3 min read

What Instagram does well

Fast posting, reels, offers and a feeling of your style. It is great for attention and daily updates.

What a website does better

Shows your full price list, location, timings and booking in one place, and appears in Google search. You also own it, so reach never drops.

Use both together

Put your website link in your Instagram bio and your Instagram feed on the website. Read the full comparison in Website vs Instagram Page.
